NO MOUSE // HOW

  1. Ctrl L, type, ⏎: the url bar finds open tabs, bookmarks and history too.
  2. On the page: ' and a few letters of a link, then ⏎ (or Ctrl ⏎ for a new tab).
  3. Forms: Tab between fields, Space ticks boxes, Alt ↓ opens a dropdown.
  4. Between tabs: Alt and the tab's [number] in the sidebar.
